Non-Pinwheel Cycloidal Actuator

3D-printed 15:1 cycloidal drive delivering 120 N·m for a 6-DOF arm

Team Robocon IITR

IIT Roorkee | Apr 2025 – Dec 2025

Commercial precision gearboxes for robot arm joints are expensive and heavy. I designed a 3D-printed, non-pinwheel cycloidal actuator for the shoulder and elbow joints of our 6-DOF rover arm, aiming for high torque density and backdrivability at a fraction of the cost.

15:1reduction
120 N·moutput torque
≤0.5°backlash
1.08 kgmass

Key Innovation

A non-pinwheel cycloidal profile removes the ring-pin set, cutting part count, cost and assembly effort compared with conventional pinwheel designs while keeping the high reduction and shock tolerance of a cycloidal drive.

Results

  • Lifted a 12 kg payload at 1 m reach (about 120 N·m) with full backdrivability
  • Backlash held at or below 0.5°
  • Load testing revealed eccentric-shaft failure; reinforcing the PLA shaft with a triangular bolt pattern raised load capacity at no added cost

Skills & Tools

Cycloidal gear design, FDM 3D printing, load testing, SolidWorks
