Multi-Actuator Quadruped Testbed

12-DOF quadruped to benchmark QDD, cycloidal, harmonic and capstan drives

Research Project

IIT Roorkee | Prof. P. M. Pathak | Feb 2026 – Present

Choosing a drivetrain for a legged robot is usually done on paper. This testbed lets different actuator types be compared on the same 12-DOF quadruped under real locomotion loads.

12DOF
4drive types

Approach

  • Architected a 12-DOF quadruped to benchmark QDD planetary, cycloidal, harmonic and capstan drives on a single platform
  • Evaluating each drivetrain on torque density, efficiency, backdrivability and impact resilience for dynamic legged locomotion
  • Integrated compliant leg linkages for impact absorption and ground-contact stability, validating range of motion and gait geometry

Skills & Tools

Legged robot design, actuator benchmarking, compliant mechanisms, SolidWorks
