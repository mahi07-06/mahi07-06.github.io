Automated Actuator Design Framework

Math-driven design of internal planetary and Wolfrom actuators inside frameless BLDC motors

Research Internship

Stochastic Robotics Lab, IISc Bengaluru | Prof. Shishir N. Y. Kolathaya | May 2026 – Present

Compact, high-torque-density actuators are the heart of legged and humanoid robots. This project replaces intuition-based gearbox sizing with a math-driven framework that designs actuators with the gearbox embedded inside the motor housing, minimizing axial width while maximizing torque density.

150+CAD parameters
4gearbox families
2motor types: inrunner & outrunner

Key Contributions

  • Automated design framework for internal planetary (single, compound, dual-stage) and Wolfrom (WPG) gearboxes embedded in frameless inrunner and outrunner BLDC motors
  • Math-driven optimizer built on geometric and assembly constraints, gear-strength analytics and a weighted mass–width–efficiency cost function, selecting the optimal QDD actuator for any target gear ratio
  • Automated parametric CAD generation through the Onshape API across 150+ design parameters, so every optimized design is immediately printable
  • Metal dual-stage inrunner planetary actuator currently being designed and manufactured for high-torque, thermally robust humanoid joints

Why Inrunner Motors?

Inrunner motors dissipate heat better than outrunners, which matters for humanoid joints that hold high torques for long periods. Extending the framework to inrunners, and moving from 3D-printed prototypes to metal hardware, targets exactly this use case.

Skills & Tools

Python, Onshape API, gear design, constrained optimization, DFM for 3D printing and machining
