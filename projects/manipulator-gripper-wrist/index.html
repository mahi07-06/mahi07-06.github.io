High-Payload Gripper & Differential Wrist

Lead-screw gripper and fully differential wrist handling 9–10 kg

Team Robocon IITR

IIT Roorkee | Aug 2025 – Jan 2026

The end-effector for our rover’s 6-DOF arm had to grasp heavy, irregular objects in competition tasks while staying compact.

9–10 kgpayload
11 cmgripper stroke
2-DOFdifferential wrist

Design

  • Self-centering parallel lead-screw gripper for precise, symmetric grasping of heavy payloads
  • Compact 2-DOF fully differential wrist with 3D-printed bevel gears, sharing load across both motors for pitch and roll
  • Integrated with the arm to reliably handle 9–10 kg payloads in competition manipulation tasks

Skills & Tools

Gripper design, bevel gear differentials, lead screws, 3D printing
