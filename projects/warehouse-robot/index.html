Autonomous Warehouse Robot

Rack-inventory robot with a retractable 1.8 m cascade lift for Eternal

Inter IIT Tech Meet 14.0

Problem statement by Eternal | Nov 2025 – Dec 2025

Eternal needed a robot that scans inventory on tall warehouse racks. I broke the problem down into reach, accuracy and transportability, and designed a mobile robot around a lightweight, retractable cascade lift.

1.8 mrack reach
1 cmvertical accuracy
4 cmhorizontal accuracy

Design

  • Lightweight retractable cascade lift reaches 1.8 m racks yet collapses for compact transport
  • Rigid chassis with omnidirectional mecanum-wheel drive for precise positioning in narrow aisles
  • Bearing-based anti-slip timing-belt tensioner, a reliability-vs-cost trade-off that cut long-term maintenance

Skills & Tools

Mobile robot design, lift mechanisms, belt drives, SolidWorks
