Monoped Actuator Test Bench

Sandwich-leg monoped rig to validate actuators under jumping and impact loads

Research Internship

Stochastic Robotics Lab, IISc Bengaluru | 2026

Optimized actuators need to be proven on hardware under realistic loads. I designed a fully parametric monoped test bench to compare multiple actuator designs under impact and dynamic jumping loads.

Design

  • Chain-driven sandwich-leg architecture: the leg sits between two actuators, one driving the thigh and one the shank, keeping leg inertia low and avoiding excess load on the thigh actuator
  • Vertical linear guide constrains the monoped to one degree of freedom for controlled, repeatable jumping experiments
  • Radial quick-swap coupling lets actuators be exchanged without disassembling the leg, cutting downtime between tests

Skills & Tools

Mechanism design, chain transmissions, test-rig design, SolidWorks / Onshape
