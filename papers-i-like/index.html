Papers I Like

Papers that shaped how I think about robot hardware

Actuator Design

Mini Cheetah: A Platform for Pushing the Limits of Dynamic Quadruped Control

B. Katz, J. Di Carlo, S. Kim
IEEE ICRA, 2019

The reference design for low-cost, compact QDD actuators with a single-stage planetary gearbox embedded in the motor. It shaped how I think about internal gearbox actuators.

DOI

COMPAct: Computational Optimization and Automated Modular Design of Planetary Actuators

A. Singh, D. Kapa, S. Joshi, S. Kolathaya
IEEE ICRA, 2026

Formalizes planetary actuator design as an optimization problem with automated CAD. The foundation for my internship work on internal and Wolfrom gearboxes.

arXiv

Anti-Backlash Mechanisms for Cycloidal Drive Robotic Actuators: Design and Evaluation

W. Roozing, J. Volbeda
IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters, 2025

Shows how to embed a cycloidal reducer inside an outrunner motor and preload it to cut backlash. Useful for my cycloidal actuator work.

Search

Legged Robotics

Learning Agile and Dynamic Motor Skills for Legged Robots

J. Hwangbo, J. Lee, A. Dosovitskiy, D. Bellicoso, V. Tsounis, V. Koltun, M. Hutter
Science Robotics, 2019

Showed that policies trained in simulation, with a learned actuator model, transfer to real quadruped hardware. A great example of why actuator modeling matters.

arXiv

Learning Quadrupedal Locomotion over Challenging Terrain

J. Lee, J. Hwangbo, L. Wellhausen, V. Koltun, M. Hutter
Science Robotics, 2020

Robust blind locomotion over rough terrain, which puts real impact loads on actuators and legs.

arXiv

Manipulation & Robotic Hands

Learning Dexterous In-Hand Manipulation

OpenAI et al.
International Journal of Robotics Research, 2020

Dexterous in-hand reorientation on a tendon-driven robot hand. It shows how much hand hardware design limits manipulation.
