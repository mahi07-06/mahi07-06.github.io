Project Ideas

Problems I want to work on next. If any of these interest you, let's talk!

✋ Robotic Hands

Compact Backdrivable Finger Actuators

Motivation: Most robotic hands trade strength for size: tendon-driven fingers are light but hard to control, while geared fingers are strong but bulky and not backdrivable.

Approach: Apply my actuator optimization framework to miniature planetary and Wolfrom reducers sized for finger joints, targeting high torque density with transparent, backdrivable fingers.

Challenges: Tiny gear modules, heat in small motors, wiring through the palm

Potential Impact: Strong, safe hands that can sense contact through their actuators

Underactuated Adaptive Gripper

Motivation: Grasping unknown objects in warehouses needs a gripper that adapts its shape without complex control.

Approach: Linkage-based underactuated fingers with compliant joints, driven by a single high-torque actuator.

Challenges: Grasp stability, repeatability, wear

Potential Impact: Low-cost, robust grippers for industrial automation

⚙️ Advanced Actuators

Thermally Optimized Humanoid Joint Actuator

Motivation: Humanoid joints hold high torques for long periods, so motors overheat long before gears fail.

Approach: Combine inrunner frameless motors, metal internal gearboxes and a housing designed as a heat sink, with thermal limits added to the optimizer.

Challenges: Thermal modeling, machining cost, sealing

Potential Impact: Humanoid joints that sustain higher continuous torque

Actuator Co-Design for a Full Leg

Motivation: Actuators are usually picked one joint at a time, ignoring how leg geometry changes the torque each joint needs.

Approach: Optimize gear ratios, motor choice and link lengths together for a target jump or gait.

Challenges: Large design space, accurate dynamic models

Potential Impact: Lighter legs that jump higher with the same motors

🦿 Legged Robots

Impact-Resilient Leg Transmission

Motivation: Landing impacts damage gears in 3D-printed and low-cost legs.

Approach: Add tuned compliance or a mechanical fuse in the transmission and validate it on the monoped test bench.

Challenges: Keeping control bandwidth high while adding compliance

Potential Impact: Cheaper legged robots that survive falls
