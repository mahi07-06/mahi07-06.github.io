  1. Optimization Framework for Internal Planetary Gearbox Actuators and Monoped Test Bench
    Mahiraj Seervi
    Research report, Stochastic Robotics Lab, IISc Bengaluru, 2026 In Preparation

    A computational optimization and automated design framework for internal planetary gearbox actuators, where the gearbox is embedded within the motor housing to reduce axial width. The framework supports inrunner and outrunner frameless BLDC motors, optimizes gearbox parameters to minimize mass and maximize efficiency, and generates parametric CAD for direct fabrication. A monoped leg and actuator test bench were designed for rapid, repeatable hardware validation.
