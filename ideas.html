Future Directions

Problems I want to work on next. If you are working on any of these, I would love to talk.

✋

Dexterous Robotic Hands

Compact, backdrivable finger actuation for robotic hands: tendon-driven and miniature geared designs that balance grip strength, speed and robustness to contact.

🤖

Humanoid Joint Actuators

High-torque, thermally robust actuators for humanoid joints, moving optimized designs from 3D-printed prototypes to metal hardware.

📐

Actuator–Robot Co-Design

Optimizing actuators together with leg and arm geometry for the task, instead of picking motors and gearboxes in isolation.

🦿

Impact-Resilient Legged Robots

Drivetrains and compliant leg mechanisms that survive jumping and landing loads while staying lightweight.

🏭

Industrial Automation

Reliable, maintainable mechanisms for warehouse and factory robots, such as lifts, grippers and mobile platforms.

⚙️

Novel Transmissions

Wolfrom, compound planetary and cycloidal variants that push torque density while keeping backlash low and assembly simple.
