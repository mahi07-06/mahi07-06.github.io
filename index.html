I'm a third-year Mechanical Engineering undergraduate at IIT Roorkee who loves designing and building the hardware that makes robots move. I'm currently a Research Intern at the Stochastic Robotics Lab, IISc Bengaluru, and the Mechanical Head of Team Robocon IITR.

My work focuses on robotic actuator design, legged robotics and robotic manipulation. I've built math-driven design frameworks for compact planetary and Wolfrom actuators, high-torque 3D-printed cycloidal drives, a multi-actuator quadruped testbed, and Mars rovers that placed in the top tier at international competitions.

Research Interests

  • Actuator Design: Quasi-direct-drive (QDD) actuators, internal planetary and Wolfrom gearboxes, cycloidal drives and compact high-torque-density systems
  • Legged Robotics: Quadruped and humanoid hardware, drivetrain selection and impact-resilient leg design
  • Robotic Manipulation & Hands: Grippers, differential wrists and dexterous robotic hands with compact, strong finger actuation
  • Co-Design Optimization: Replacing intuition-based sizing with optimization that couples actuator, mechanism and task

Current Work

  • Stochastic Robotics Lab, IISc: Developing an automated design framework for internal planetary and Wolfrom actuators embedded in frameless BLDC motors, and designing and manufacturing a metal dual-stage inrunner actuator for humanoid joints
  • IIT Roorkee: Building a 12-DOF multi-actuator quadruped testbed to benchmark QDD planetary, cycloidal, harmonic and capstan drives with Prof. P. M. Pathak
  • Team Robocon IITR: Leading the mechanical division building our Mars rover for international rover challenges

selected publications

  1. Optimization Framework for Internal Planetary Gearbox Actuators and Monoped Test Bench
    Mahiraj Seervi
    Research report, Stochastic Robotics Lab, IISc Bengaluru, 2026 In Preparation

    A computational optimization and automated design framework for internal planetary gearbox actuators, where the gearbox is embedded within the motor housing to reduce axial width. The framework supports inrunner and outrunner frameless BLDC motors, optimizes gearbox parameters to minimize mass and maximize efficiency, and generates parametric CAD for direct fabrication. A monoped leg and actuator test bench were designed for rapid, repeatable hardware validation.
