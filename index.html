Mahiraj Seervi

Robotic Actuators · Legged Robots · Manipulation
B.Tech. Mechanical Engineering, IIT Roorkee · Class of 2028

I am a third-year mechanical engineering undergraduate at IIT Roorkee who designs and builds the hardware that makes robots move: high-torque-density actuators, legged platforms and manipulators. My work spans the full loop, from math-driven design optimization and parametric CAD to fabrication, testing and iteration on real hardware.

I am currently a Research Intern at the Stochastic Robotics Lab, IISc Bengaluru, working on actuator co-design for legged and humanoid robots, and the Mechanical Head of Team Robocon IITR, where I lead the mechanical division building our Mars rover.

◆Research Interests

⚙️

Actuator Design

Quasi-direct-drive, planetary, Wolfrom and cycloidal actuators with high torque density, low backlash and backdrivability.

🦿

Legged Robotics

Quadrupeds and humanoids: drivetrain selection, compliant legs and hardware that survives impacts.

✋

Robotic Manipulation & Hands

Grippers, wrists and dexterous robotic hands, with compact, strong and compliant finger actuation.

📐

Co-Design Optimization

Replacing intuition-based sizing with optimization that couples actuator, mechanism and task.

🔩

Mechanism Design

Suspensions, lifts, linkages and transmissions designed for manufacturability and reliability.

🛰️

Field & Space Robotics

Planetary rovers, sampling mechanisms and robots that work on rough terrain.

◆Current Work

  • Research Intern, Stochastic Robotics Lab, IISc (Prof. Shishir N. Y. Kolathaya): developing an automated design framework for internal planetary and Wolfrom actuators embedded in frameless BLDC motors, and building a metal dual-stage inrunner actuator for humanoid joints.
  • Multi-Actuator Quadruped Testbed (Prof. P. M. Pathak, IIT Roorkee): a 12-DOF platform to benchmark QDD planetary, cycloidal, harmonic and capstan drives.
  • Mechanical Head, Team Robocon IITR: leading a 40+ member mechanical division building a Mars rover for international competitions.
